My Writing Philosophy

Writing is where I put the things I can't say out loud — a quiet place to grumble about someone or something I'd never say to their face. Sometimes it's just me trying to think more clearly. And every now and then, almost by accident, something here turns out to be useful to someone else.

“Writing is thinking. To write well is to think clearly. That's why it's so hard.” — David McCullough
